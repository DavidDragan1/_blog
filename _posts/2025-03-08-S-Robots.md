With all the latest advancements, every new iteration becomes increasingly underwhelming. The big boom has happened, and we are on the other side of it now - at a plateau. 

This is not in any way to discount the good work of the innovators now pulling the grunt work for 0.01% improvements - it is that itself which *creates* the next boom. The 0.01% refinements are necessary to uncover the big breaks.

The angle I am discussing here is one more... abstract in nature: Sex Robots will catapult us into the next wave of advancement. 

# The narrative is simple enough
Some of the best programmers, hackers, and most brilliant minds in tech suffer from one common problem - that of loneliness. All their lives they've been shunned from society, laughed at, bullied and abused - they've had to endure "freak" treatment for their entire lives, technology being their only escape. Computers were the only things in the world they could communicate with that seemed to understand them and accept them for who they are, extending no judgement other than pointing out code errors.

Computers were an easy friend, one that let them explore the world - one who would relay knowledge and connect them to other people similarly shunned. 

By one way or another, some of these people came to excel at what they do, but still it is that nagging, deep-seated, primal *yearn* for a human connection that beckons them, never too far away to plunge them back to the depths of despair.
